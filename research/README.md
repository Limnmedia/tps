# Research

Research is the working space for investigating, experimenting, challenging assumptions, and developing the mathematics behind TPS.

Research is separate from the specification:

- Research asks: Is this true? Why does it work? Under what conditions?
- The specification states: This is how TPS is defined, these are its constraints, and these are its validated mathematical relationships.
- Education explains: Here is how TPS can be understood and used.

The research process may progress from observation to hypothesis, derivation, experiment, validation, and specification. Not every research finding needs to become part of the specification.

Research should preserve its history, including unsuccessful experiments and rejected formulations. Such records explain the decisions that shape the specification.

The subdirectories are reserved for references, derivations, experiments, and papers. They are intentionally unpopulated until research is ready to be added.
